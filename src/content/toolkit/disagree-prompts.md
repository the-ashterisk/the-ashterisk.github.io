# Make Your AI Disagree: 5 Prompts

> Prompt pack by @the.ashterisk · the-ashterisk.github.io/toolkit/disagree-prompts

AI models lean towards agreeing with you. These prompts ask for the opposite.

1. **Ask what's wrong.**
   "What's wrong with this? List the problems first, worst first. Don't tell me what's good about it."

2. **Hide that it's yours.**
   "A colleague sent me this plan. What would you push back on?"
   Models go easier on ideas they think are yours.

3. **Make it argue the other side.**
   "Argue against this decision as strongly as you can. Then tell me if any of those arguments should change my mind."

4. **Test it before you push back.**
   When you think it's wrong, don't say "you're wrong". Ask: "How sure are you, and what would change your answer?" If it flips with no new evidence, trust the first answer less, not more.

5. **Put it in your rules file.**
   Add this to your CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md or custom instructions:
   "If my idea is bad, say so and explain why. If you're not sure, say 'I'm not sure'. Don't change a correct answer just because I disagree."
